Privacy Policy — Novacula

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Versión en español

Summary

Novacula requires no account and processes tool content on your device. Kolino Labs does not receive your photos, QR codes, passwords, location data, or ICE card information. The app contains no ads. The QR scanner uses Google ML Kit, which may contact Google servers and send technical diagnostic and usage data as explained below. Core features work without an internet connection; the Network Scanner needs access to your local WiFi network.

QR scanner diagnostics

Novacula integrates Google ML Kit on Android and iOS to recognize QR codes. According to Google, the SDK may collect device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, usage events, and error codes. On Android, it may also collect feature input and output sizes and the feature version. Google uses this data for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypts it in transit with HTTPS, and states that it does not share it with third parties. Kolino Labs does not receive these diagnostics. ML Kit processes images and QR content on the device; Google states that it does not send that content or scan results to its servers. The SDK may also contact Google for fixes and model or hardware compatibility updates.

Sources: ML Kit privacy, Android data disclosure, and iOS data disclosure.

Data Kolino Labs does not receive

Kolino Labs operates no app servers and receives no Novacula usage data. If you choose to share a file or open a link, the operating system hands that action to the app or service you select.

Device permissions and what they are used for

  • Camera — scanning QR codes, the magnifier and the mirror. Images are processed on-device and are never saved or sent; ML Kit may send the technical data described above.
  • Microphone — measuring ambient noise in decibels. Audio is analyzed in real time and never recorded.
  • Location (while using the app only) — the operating system requires it to read WiFi network details (WiFi Traffic Light / Network Scanner). Your position is never logged or transmitted.
  • Local network — the Network Scanner analyzes only your own WiFi network to list connected devices. Results stay on your phone.
  • Photos — the Metadata Cleaner reads the image you choose in order to strip its metadata (GPS, camera, date) and share a clean copy. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Motion sensors — bubble level and compass.
  • Exact alarms (Android) — scheduling the Rescue Call.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences (favorites, tool settings, ICE medical card) are stored only in your phone's local storage and are deleted when you uninstall the app. The ICE card contains information you enter voluntarily and it never leaves the device.

Children

Novacula is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not designed for children.
